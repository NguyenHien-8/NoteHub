No xay ra khi NoteHub.exe duoc chay ma khong co bo Qt + MinGW runtime o cung thu muc.

Cach build lai sach:

      -QtRoot "C:\Qt\6.11.2\mingw_64" `
      -Compiler "C:\Qt\Tools\mingw1310_64\bin\g++.exe" `
      -Clean `
      -ForceCloseRunning

  .\dist\windows\NoteHub.exe

      -QtRoot "C:\Qt\6.11.2\mingw_64" `
      -Compiler "C:\Qt\Tools\mingw1310_64\bin\g++.exe" `
      -AppDirectory "dist\windows"
